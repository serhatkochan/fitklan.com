Have another question? ↗
Can I sign in to the app from this page?+

This page presents the Fitklan product experience through interactive demos. Demo logs are not saved to a real account. For app access and ways to work with us, email business@fitklan.com.

Do I have to work with a coach?+

Fitklan brings together three paths: training independently with AI support, personal coaching and moving forward with a clan. You can use them together. AI support and professional coaching are separate experiences.

Does AI replace my coach?+

AI is designed to help prepare logs, explanations and review drafts. In personal coaching, plan decisions and professional assessments remain under your coach’s control. AI responses and coach notes are shown separately.

Do I have to use voice, a camera or calorie tracking?+

No. Voice logging is optional; you can complete the same flow on screen. The nutrition experience is designed to support logging with a photo, short note or quantity. This landing page does not request camera or microphone access.

How can coaches and dietitians get involved?+

Coaches and coaching teams working online, in person or in a hybrid setup can contact us. The dietitian experience is built around a dedicated workspace for professionals whose credentials have been verified. Tell us your area of interest and how you work in your email.

Can my clan see all my logs?+

The planned sharing model is based on your preferences. Private workout logs, progress photos and conversations with your coach are not automatically shared with your clan. AI clan summaries are limited to content shared within that community.
